# Shipping Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines delivery expectations and the procedures customer support should follow when customers ask about shipment status, delays, or delivery estimates.

## Standard Delivery

Standard domestic shipments are normally delivered within 3-5 business days from the shipment date.

The promised delivery date shown to the customer should be treated as the primary delivery commitment.

## Express Delivery

Express shipments are normally delivered within 1-2 business days.

If an express shipment exceeds the promised delivery date, customer support should investigate the shipment immediately.

## Delayed Shipments

A shipment is considered delayed when the current date is later than the promised delivery date and the shipment has not been delivered.

When a customer reports a delayed shipment, customer support should:

1. Verify the order number.
2. Verify the shipment status.
3. Check the promised delivery date.
4. Determine the number of business days the shipment is delayed.
5. Provide the customer with the latest available estimated delivery date.
6. Explain the reason for the delay when that information is available.

## Escalation Threshold

Shipments delayed by more than 5 business days should be escalated to the logistics support team.

The escalation should include:

- Customer ID
- Order ID
- Shipment ID
- Original promised delivery date
- Current shipment status
- Number of business days delayed
- Latest estimated delivery date

## International Shipments

International shipments may require additional processing time because of customs clearance, security inspections, or local carrier processing.

Customer support should not promise a specific international delivery date unless a confirmed carrier estimate is available.

## Customer Communication

When communicating a shipment delay, support representatives should provide factual information and avoid guaranteeing a delivery date that has not been confirmed by the carrier.
