# Lost Shipment Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how customer support should identify, investigate, and resolve shipments that may be lost during transportation.

## Definition of a Lost Shipment

A shipment should not be classified as lost solely because it is delayed.

A shipment may be considered potentially lost when:

- The carrier has explicitly reported the shipment as lost.
- Tracking information has not changed for an extended period and the carrier investigation indicates a potential loss.
- The carrier investigation has confirmed that the package cannot be located.

Customer support must rely on carrier confirmation before classifying a shipment as officially lost.

## Initial Investigation

When a customer reports a potentially lost shipment, customer support should:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Verify the shipment ID.
3. Review the latest tracking status.
4. Check the last known tracking event.
5. Check the expected delivery date.
6. Determine whether the carrier has reported the shipment as lost.
7. Create or update a carrier investigation when appropriate.

## Delayed Versus Lost

A delayed shipment and a lost shipment must be treated differently.

A shipment that is late but still moving through the carrier network should remain classified as delayed.

A shipment should only be classified as lost after sufficient investigation or carrier confirmation.

This distinction is important because refund and replacement eligibility may depend on the official shipment status.

## Replacement

When a shipment is confirmed as lost, the customer may be eligible for a replacement depending on inventory availability and applicable order terms.

Before offering a replacement, support should verify:

- Order status
- Shipment status
- Product availability
- Customer eligibility
- Applicable replacement rules

## Refund

A confirmed lost shipment may qualify for a refund.

Before processing a refund, support should verify that the carrier has confirmed the shipment as lost and that the order has not already been refunded or replaced.

## Duplicate Resolution

Customer support must check whether a refund or replacement has already been issued.

A customer should not receive both a replacement and a full refund unless an authorized exception has been approved.

## Escalation

Escalate the case when:

- The carrier investigation is inconclusive.
- The customer disputes the carrier status.
- The order has high-value items.
- A replacement is unavailable.
- A previous refund or replacement exists.
- Fraud or suspicious activity is suspected.

## Customer Communication

Support representatives should explain the current investigation status clearly.

If the shipment is still under investigation, representatives should not tell the customer that the shipment is definitely lost.

Customers should be provided with the latest confirmed information and the next expected action.
