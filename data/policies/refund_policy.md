# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when customers may receive a refund for shipping-related issues, canceled orders, or service failures.

## General Refund Eligibility

A customer may be eligible for a refund when:

- The order was canceled before fulfillment.
- The shipment was lost by the carrier.
- The shipment was significantly delayed and the applicable service commitment was not met.
- The customer received a damaged package and the claim is approved.
- The customer was incorrectly charged for a service.

Refund eligibility must be verified against the order, shipment status, and applicable policy.

## Delayed Shipment Refunds

A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund.

Before approving a refund for a delayed shipment, customer support must:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Verify the shipment ID.
3. Confirm the promised delivery date.
4. Determine the number of business days delayed.
5. Check whether the shipment has been delivered.
6. Determine whether the delay qualifies under the applicable service commitment.

Shipments delayed by more than 5 business days should be reviewed by the logistics support team before a refund is approved.

## Lost Shipment Refunds

If a shipment is confirmed as lost by the carrier, the customer may be eligible for a refund of the applicable order amount.

Customer support must verify that the carrier has classified the shipment as lost before processing the refund.

A shipment that is simply delayed must not be classified as lost without carrier confirmation.

## Damaged Package Refunds

For damaged packages, the customer should provide evidence of the damage when required.

The support representative should create a damage claim and follow the damaged package procedure before approving a refund.

## Cancellation Refunds

If an order is canceled before fulfillment, the customer may qualify for a full refund.

If fulfillment has already started, the cancellation may require additional review.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount depends on the applicable policy and the amount originally charged to the customer.

Shipping charges may be refundable when the applicable service commitment was not met.

Customer support must not promise a specific refund amount until the order and applicable policy have been verified.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds should be submitted through the authorized refund process.

Customers should be informed that the time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment provider.

## Escalation

Refund requests should be escalated when:

- The refund amount exceeds the standard support authorization limit.
- The customer disputes a previous refund decision.
- The order has conflicting shipment or payment information.
- Fraud or suspicious activity is suspected.
