# Damaged Package Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how customer support should handle orders where the customer reports that a package or product was damaged during delivery.

## Reporting Damage

Customers should report damaged packages as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

Customer support should collect:

- Order ID
- Shipment ID
- Product information
- Delivery date
- Description of the damage
- Photographs or other evidence when required
- Packaging condition when relevant

## Initial Assessment

Customer support should determine whether the damage appears to involve:

- External packaging damage
- Product damage
- Missing components
- Incorrect item
- Visible signs of carrier mishandling

The support representative should record the customer's description accurately and avoid making a final determination before the claim is reviewed.

## Evidence Requirements

Evidence may be required before a replacement or refund is approved.

Acceptable evidence may include:

- Photographs of the damaged product
- Photographs of the shipping box
- Photographs of the shipping label
- Description of missing or damaged components

The exact evidence requirements may depend on the product and order type.

## Replacement

If a damage claim is approved, the customer may be eligible for a replacement when inventory is available.

Before creating a replacement, support should verify:

1. The order ID.
2. The shipment ID.
3. The product involved.
4. The damage claim status.
5. Product availability.
6. Whether a previous replacement or refund has already been issued.

## Refund

An approved damaged-package claim may qualify for a refund.

The refund amount should be determined according to the applicable order and refund rules.

Customer support should not promise a refund before the damage claim has been reviewed when review is required.

## Carrier Damage

When the damage appears to have occurred during transportation, customer support may need to create a carrier damage claim.

The carrier claim process should not prevent the customer from receiving an eligible resolution under the applicable customer service policy.

## Multiple Claims

If a customer reports multiple damaged products from the same order, each affected item should be documented.

Support should verify whether the issue is related to a single shipment or multiple shipments.

## Escalation

Escalate the case when:

- The damage claim involves a high-value product.
- Evidence is insufficient but the customer disputes the decision.
- The customer has already received a refund or replacement.
- The customer disputes the damage assessment.
- Carrier liability is unclear.
- Fraud or suspicious activity is suspected.

## Customer Communication

Support representatives should explain:

- What information is required
- Whether the claim is under review
- What resolution options may be available
- What the next step is

Representatives should avoid guaranteeing a refund or replacement until eligibility has been confirmed.
