# Cancellation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the rules for canceling customer orders and determining whether a customer is eligible for a cancellation refund.

## Cancellation Before Fulfillment

Customers may request cancellation before an order enters the fulfillment process.

When an order has not entered fulfillment, customer support may approve the cancellation according to the customer's order status and applicable terms.

If the cancellation is approved before fulfillment, the customer is generally eligible for a refund of the amount charged.

## Cancellation After Fulfillment Begins

Once fulfillment has started, cancellation may no longer be available through standard customer support.

Customer support should:

1. Check the current order status.
2. Determine whether fulfillment has started.
3. Check whether the shipment has already been created.
4. Determine whether the order can still be canceled.
5. Escalate when standard cancellation is no longer available.

Customers should not be promised a cancellation after fulfillment has started unless the system confirms that cancellation is possible.

## Cancellation After Shipment

Orders that have already been shipped generally cannot be canceled directly.

The customer may need to:

- Refuse delivery when supported by the carrier.
- Request a return after delivery.
- Contact customer support for further instructions.

The applicable return or refund policy should be followed.

## Cancellation Fees

Some specialized services or orders may have cancellation fees.

Customer support must verify the order terms before confirming whether a cancellation fee applies.

Support representatives should not assume that all cancellations are free.

## Refund Processing

When a cancellation is approved and a refund is applicable, the refund should be submitted through the authorized refund process.

The refund amount should be based on the actual amount charged and any applicable cancellation terms.

## Duplicate Cancellation Requests

If a customer submits multiple cancellation requests for the same order, support should verify the order status before taking additional action.

A completed cancellation should not be processed again.

## Escalation

Cancellation requests should be escalated when:

- Fulfillment status is unclear.
- The customer disputes a cancellation fee.
- Payment information conflicts with the order record.
- The order has already shipped but the customer requests cancellation.
- The customer requests an exception to standard cancellation rules.

## Customer Communication

Customer support should clearly explain whether the cancellation was:

- Approved
- Rejected
- Pending review
- Escalated

If a refund is approved, the customer should also be informed that processing time may depend on the payment provider.
